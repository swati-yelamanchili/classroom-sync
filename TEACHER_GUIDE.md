# Classroom Sync: Teacher Guide

Your student sees Ashray Akruti's sign language video and script for the topic you are teaching, and their screen changes when you move to the next topic.

**You need:** a laptop with Google Chrome, and a phone or tablet for each student, all on the same Wi-Fi with internet access (the videos play from Ashray Akruti's YouTube channel).

## Before class (5 minutes)

1. **Open the app** on your laptop in Google Chrome, and sign in with the passcode.
2. **Create the lesson:** press **New lesson** and type the topics you will teach, one per line, using the lesson names from the YouTube videos (for example "Waste Management"). Press **Find resources**.
3. **Check the list.** Each topic shows the video and script found for it.
   - **"No matching resource found"**: press **Replace** to choose one, or keep the topic without one.
   - **"Possible match: please check"**: press **Preview** to make sure it is the right video.
   - Use the up and down arrows to change the order. The numbers shown are the numbers you will say in class.
4. **Read each lesson plan:** press **Lesson plan** on a topic. It has 5 parts, written by AI from the video's captions, so check it makes sense. If it says it could not be made, press **Try again** or teach from the video.
5. **Choose Voice Trigger mode** and press **Confirm sequence**.

## Start the class

6. **Connect the student:** on the student's phone, open the **Student link** shown on your screen, or go to the app's **/join** page and type the **session code**. The student sees "Waiting for your teacher".
7. Check **Student devices** shows the right number.
8. Press **Start session**, then **Enable microphone**. Allow the microphone if Chrome asks.

## While teaching

Say a command **on its own, with a short pause before and after**:

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Start Topic 3" | Student sees Topic 3 |
| "Next Topic" / "Previous Topic" | Moves one topic |
| "Repeat Topic 2" | Goes back to Topic 2 |
| "Pause Session" / "Resume Session" | Pauses or continues |

- Normal teaching speech is ignored. "Today we will start topic three" does **not** change anything.
- After each command, the panel shows **"Command recognised"** and what changed. If it shows **"Command rejected"**, it tells you why.
- **If voice does not work, use the buttons.** **Next topic**, **Previous topic** and the topic list always work.

**Students following the lesson plan on their own phone** (no code needed): they open **/lessons**, choose your lesson and press **Listen for my teacher**. Say **"Part 3"** (it works mid-sentence), **"next part"** or **"previous part"**, and their page jumps to that part of the plan. "Start Topic 2" changes topic there too. They can also tap a part, or press **Show transcript with timestamps** to read the video's captions.

## End the class

9. Press **End session**. The student sees "This lesson has ended". The lesson is saved, so you can **Reuse** it next time.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The student's screen shows a red "Connection lost" bar | Wait. It reconnects and catches up on its own. Check the phone's Wi-Fi. |
| The student link does not open on the phone | Make sure the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop. Ask whoever set up the app to allow it through the laptop's firewall. |
| "Microphone permission denied" | Click the lock icon in Chrome's address bar, allow the microphone, then press **Enable microphone** again. |
| Commands are often missed | Speak a little slower, pause before and after, and move closer to the laptop. Or use the buttons. |
| A topic changes when you did not mean it to | In **Commands and settings**, tick **Only respond after the word "Classroom"**. Then say "Classroom, next topic". |
| The video does not play | Check the phone can open youtube.com. Then press **Open video in a new tab** under the video. |
| The video is in the wrong language | Before class, press **Replace** on that topic and choose the version in your class's language. |

**Privacy:** the app does not record your voice. Chrome sends microphone audio to Google to turn it into text, so use voice only with your school's permission. Students do not need an account, and no student information is stored.
